Walk into every visit already prepared. With pre-charting in Doximity Scribe , you can add context before the encounter by typing, dictating, or pasting it in, and Doximity Scribe folds that information into the note it generates. The relevant history, the plan you already have in mind, the details you do not want to lose: capture them up front and let them shape the documentation.
