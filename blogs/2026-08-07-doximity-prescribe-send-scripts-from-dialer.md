Doximity Prescribe is a new way to send prescriptions electronically, right from the Dialer workflow you already use to connect with patients. Open Dialer and tap the Rx icon to send a script in minutes — no logging into your EMR, no separate app. Your patient can choose their preferred pharmacy by text, or you can select one for them.
