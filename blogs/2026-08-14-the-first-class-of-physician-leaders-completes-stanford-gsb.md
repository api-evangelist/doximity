Fewer than 5% of US health systems are led by physician CEOs. Physicians spend their careers making consequential decisions under pressure, earning the trust of patients and families, and seeing firsthand where our healthcare system works—and where it doesn’t. Yet they remain underrepresented in the most senior leadership roles of the organizations where care is delivered.
