Doximity Ask now retrieves the latest clinical trial data from ClinicalTrials.gov, delivering more complete and accurate answers about ongoing and unpublished studies, including data such as recruiting status and trial outcomes. Preliminary trial data is also clearly flagged in our answers, so clinicians know the strength of evidence behind each answer.
