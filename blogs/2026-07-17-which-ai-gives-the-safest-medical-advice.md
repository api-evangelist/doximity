This week, the independent clinical AI research group ARISE released NOHARM v2 , a study evaluating whether AI recommendations are safe in realistic clinical scenarios. The group is led by physicians at Stanford and Harvard. Doximity Ask received the highest score in real-world testing of commercially available products by physicians (75.8%), ahead of ChatGPT Health (68.4%) and OpenEvidence (60.8%).
