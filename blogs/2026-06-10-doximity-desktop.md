Doximity Desktop is a native Mac and Windows app that brings the full Clinical AI Suite (Ask, Scribe, and Dialer) to the physician workstation. It lives in the taskbar, opens Ask with a keystroke from anywhere, and lets you copy answers and documentation straight into the EHR. It is free for verified US physicians, NPs, and PAs, HIPAA-compliant under BAA, and deployable by enterprise IT.
