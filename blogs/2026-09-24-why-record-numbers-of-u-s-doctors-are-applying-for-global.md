For eight years, the Doximity Foundation has provided financial support for U.S. doctors partnering with health organizations around the world. So far this year, we've supported 633 clinician trips to 59 countries from Ghana to Guatemala, where they provide essential, and sometimes lifesaving, care to countless patients.
