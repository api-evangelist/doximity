Some clinical questions have several factors to weigh: a patient whose comorbidities pull in different directions, a presentation that could go more than one way, a page of labs that only resolves when you read it as a whole. These have to be worked through. Today, we are introducing Thinking Mode in Doximity Ask , built specifically for questions that need deeper reasoning.
