Doximity's 2026 Physician Compensation Report finds AI fluency emerging as a potential career advantage, even as overwork and early-retirement intent climb. Physician compensation rose just 2% from 2024 to 2025, its slowest growth in several years. But the forces reshaping physicians' economic lives are moving much faster.
